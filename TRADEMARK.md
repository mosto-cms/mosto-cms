# Name and logo policy

The code of Mosto CMS is MIT licensed: you can use, change and redistribute it freely.
The license covers the code, **not the name or the logo**.

- "Mosto CMS" and the Mosto CMS logo identify the official releases published from this
  repository and its npm scope `@mosto-cms`.
- You may say that your product or site "uses Mosto CMS" or is "built with Mosto CMS".
- If you publish a modified fork, give it a different name and do not suggest it is the
  official project or endorsed by Mosto.
- The admin shows a small "Powered by Mosto CMS" link. You can turn it off; you do not need
  permission to do so.
