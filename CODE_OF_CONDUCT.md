# Code of Conduct

Mosto CMS adopts the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/)
as its code of conduct, for every space of the project: issues, pull requests, discussions and
any other channel run by the maintainers.

In short: be welcoming and respectful, assume good intent, give and accept constructive
feedback, and focus on what is best for the community. Harassment, insults, personal or
political attacks, and publishing others' private information are not acceptable.

## Reporting

Report unacceptable behaviour privately to the maintainers at **info@mosto.studio**. All
reports are reviewed promptly and handled confidentially. Maintainers who do not follow the
code of conduct in good faith may face consequences decided by the other maintainers.

## Enforcement

Maintainers apply the
[Contributor Covenant enforcement guidelines](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/#enforcement-guidelines):
correction, warning, temporary ban, permanent ban, depending on the impact of the behaviour.
